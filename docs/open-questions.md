# Open Questions & Regulatory Unknowns Register

**Repository:** `medical_portal`  
**Purpose:** Working Rule 6 establishes that the engineering team must never invent external regulatory specifications, Ministry of Health (MoH) policies, or third-party integration contracts. All unknowns are logged in this register, built behind clean abstractions with mock implementations and contract tests, and resolved through formal stakeholder engagements.

---

## 🏛️ 1. Ministry of Health (MoH) & National Regulatory Questions

### 1.1 National EMR Mandate vs. OpenMRS Platform
- **Question:** Are secondary and tertiary hospitals in Ethiopia legally or operationally mandated to deploy the national SmartCare-based EMR, or is deploying a modernized OpenMRS 3.x (O3) platform on PostgreSQL approved as a compliant EMR tier?
- **Impact on Architecture:** Governs whether Phase 3/8 targets OpenMRS 3.x directly or whether an adapter for SmartCare / Ethiopia eHealth Architecture must be maintained.
- **Interim Action:** Implement clinical data exchange strictly via standard HL7 FHIR R4 JSON REST endpoints (`Patient`, `Encounter`, `Observation`, `Condition`, `MedicationRequest`, `AllergyIntolerance`), which can interface with any compliant clinical core.

### 1.2 DHIS2 Reporting Topology (Direct vs. Offline Intermediate)
- **Question:** Does the local facility server transmit routine HMIS aggregate reports (`/api/dataValueSets`) directly to the national cloud DHIS2 instance over the internet, or is data submitted to an offline or local woreda/zonal health bureau DHIS2 node?
- **Impact on Architecture:** Influences offline batch synchronization timing, VPN/network routing, and whether direct API submission or an offline JSON file export is the primary operational mode.
- **Interim Action:** Build the DHIS2 exporter with a dual-mode workflow: (a) Automated REST push with stored import summaries for connected hospitals, and (b) Cryptographically signed JSON export file download for air-gapped or offline facilities.

### 1.3 DHIS2 Ethiopian Calendar Period Encoding
- **Question:** How does the national Ethiopian DHIS2 instance encode reporting periods? Does it expect Gregorian period strings (e.g., `202607` for July 2026, `2026Q3`) or localized Ethiopian fiscal period identifiers (e.g., `2018Hamle`, `2018Q1` for the fiscal year starting Hamle 1)?
- **Impact on Architecture:** Determines the aggregation bucket boundaries and period format strings generated by the aggregate indicator extraction engine.
- **Interim Action:** Parameterize period formatting within the DHIS2 indicator mapping configuration layer; implement a configurable period-mapper service supporting both ISO Gregorian and localized Ethiopian period syntax.

### 1.4 National Patient Identifier (Fayya / National ID) & Master Patient Index (MPI)
- **Question:** What is the technical status and API availability of Ethiopia’s national digital ID (*Fayya*) and national Master Patient Index (MPI)? What identifier format (URI scheme, barcode format) is mandated for hospital registration cards?
- **Impact on Architecture:** Dictates whether cross-facility duplicate resolution occurs purely locally via probabilistic matching or synchronizes against a national MPI server.
- **Interim Action:** Model patient identifiers with facility-scoped system URIs (e.g., `http://hospital.et/mrn/ADOT`) alongside a reserved namespace for national identifiers (`http://moh.gov.et/national-id`). Build a local duplicate-resolution conflict queue.

### 1.5 eCHIS Community Health Referral Integration
- **Question:** What is the formal message specification, authentication scheme, and regulatory approval process for bi-directional referrals between hospital EHRs and the national electronic Community Health Information System (eCHIS)?
- **Impact on Architecture:** Governs the structure of inbound referral intake and outbound referral discharge summaries.
- **Interim Action:** Model eCHIS referrals using the HL7 FHIR International Patient Summary (IPS) and FHIR `ServiceRequest` profiles behind an `IEchisClient` interface with a full fake implementation and sandbox contract tests.

### 1.6 Digital Health Software & Medical Device Certification
- **Question:** Does clinical decision support (drug interactions, allergy screening) or patient-facing diagnostic software require medical device registration or clinical software certification from the **Ethiopian Food and Drug Authority (EFDA)** or the **Ministry of Innovation and Technology (MInT)**?
- **Impact on Architecture:** Dictates safety categorization, audit retention rules, and whether clinical algorithms must be registered with national health authorities.
- **Interim Action:** Prohibit uncertified decision-support claims in the UI and documentation (SAFE-01). Treat decision support as advisory clinical reference information requiring explicit clinician override and justification.

### 1.7 Data Protection & Cross-Border Residency
- **Question:** Under the **Ethiopian Personal Data Protection Proclamation**, what specific statutory restrictions exist regarding the off-site storage, replication, or processing of patient health information (PHI) outside of Ethiopian territorial boundaries?
- **Impact on Architecture:** Enforces whether cloud aggregation and backup replication vaults must reside strictly within Ethiopian data centers (e.g., Ethio Telecom Cloud / Raxio Data Centre Addis Ababa).
- **Interim Action:** Implement on-premises hospital LAN isolation as the primary deployment target, with zero external cloud dependencies or telemetry egress.

## 🩺 3. Clinical Decision-Support & Validation Unknowns

### 3.1 National Essential Medicine List (EML) & RxNorm Mapping
- **Question:** Ethiopia publishes the *National Essential Medicine List (EML)* using generic pharmaceutical names and strength formulations rather than US National Library of Medicine (NLM) RxNorm Concept Unique Identifiers (RxCUIs). How are national medications codified across Ethiopian public facilities?
- **Impact on Architecture:** Governs whether clinical decision-support engines evaluate drug-drug interactions via RxNorm codes, Anatomical Therapeutic Chemical (ATC) classification codes, or national formulary identifiers.
- **Interim Action:** Maintain `MedicationConcept` with support for both RxNorm and ATC taxonomies. Structure knowledge base interfaces (`IInteractionKnowledgeBase`, `IAllergyKnowledgeBase`) to evaluate medications using generic international nonproprietary names (INN) and ATC classes.

### 3.2 Clinical Terminology Translation Quality
- **Question:** What validated medical glossaries exist for translating complex clinical findings, pharmaceutical instructions, and diagnostic terminology into Amharic (*አማርኛ*) and Afaan Oromoo (*Afaan Oromoo*)?
- **Impact on Architecture:** Inaccurate clinical translation presents severe patient-safety hazards (e.g. incorrect dosage frequencies or warning misinterpretations).
- **Interim Action:** Establish a formal multi-stage translation workflow: Initial professional linguistic translation &rarr; Clinical review by certified Ethiopian physicians and pharmacists &rarr; Audit trail recording translator and medical reviewer identities.
